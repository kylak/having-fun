1 Thess 5:23:

"Αὐτὸς δὲ ὁ Θεὸς τῆς εἰρήνης ἁγιάσαι ὑμᾶς ὁλοτελεῖς, καὶ ὁλόκληρον ὑμῶν τὸ πνεῦμα καὶ ἡ ψυχὴ καὶ τὸ σῶμα ἀμέμπτως ἐν τῇ παρουσίᾳ τοῦ Κυρίου ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ τηρηθείη." (f35)

"And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ." (KJV)

Steven Anderson on his sermon on 1 Thess 5:23 said that as a Christian we should take care of our body by eating well and doing some physical exercices, and that we should take care of our soul by having a social life, enjoying what life has to offer such as eating very good food sometime, or enjoying good music, arts, and so on. I think that's the way how S.A understands reserving blameless the body and the soul in 1 Thess 5:23.

So let's see if that understanding is consistent.
